Questions, answered

What documents do I need?

A government photo ID and a selfie for first-time renters. Verification is saved to your profile, so you only do it once.

How does the security deposit work?

The deposit is held at payment and refunded after the return inspection, minus any agreed deductions for damage or late return.

Can I extend a rental?

Yes, from My Bookings. Extensions are approved only if nobody has booked the gear for the extra days.

What if the gear I want is booked?

We show similar available items for your dates, so you can switch in one tap.

Can I cancel?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup. After that, a partial refund applies as per our policy.
